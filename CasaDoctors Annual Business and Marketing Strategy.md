# CasaDoctors · Home-first business and marketing plan

21 October 2026 · Vireal recommendation · supersedes the clinic-first operating plan.

## 01 Care where life happens

My recommendation: build a home-service network people can use for themselves, their family or their pets. Make appropriate video consultations the second priority.

A son is in the United States. His mother is in Egypt. She needs a suitable consultation, but arranging the visit means coordinating the right professional, her address, a time she can receive them and the payment. The son wants to help from where he is. His mother needs a service that respects her choices and privacy.

 I want CasaDoctors to make that coordination clearer. The customer chooses who needs the service and where it should happen. A qualified provider makes suitable services available within the area and hours they can actually cover. Booking and payment happen through a tested app journey; delivery happens at the supported address, or by an appropriate video consultation.

 This is the strategic direction, not a claim that every step is already built. We will first prove a small home-doctor service, then add video and other categories when their operating requirements are ready.

 Our two-sided proposition Customers arrange care where they need it. Providers choose when they are available to deliver it.

 The first three bets Why I recommend it What must be proven 

 Home services first Directly addresses travel and family-coordination needs. Qualified capacity, suitable cases, travel, household access and completed visits. 

 Video second Adds a useful option where a professional can safely deliver remotely. Scope, recipient location, consent, secure joining and failed-call recovery. 

 A local network before broad expansion A small dependable area is more useful than a broad menu with no available service. Real coverage by address and time window, support and sustainable delivery. 

 Our first 90 days will prepare the channels, recruit and activate providers, repair product gaps and run a bounded home-service pilot. The year then deepens what works, adds separately approved categories and considers an adjacent area. I am not recommending an all-category national launch.

## 05 Monthly objectives and KPI definitions

One target register powers the roadmap, monthly scorecard and annual appendix.

Measure Definition Interpretation / window 

 North-star outcome Completed eligible paid service episodes, reported separately for home healthcare, video, veterinary, pharmacy, diagnostics and commerce. Do not add orders, visits and products into an unlabeled volume total. Refund-adjusted revenue is a separate view. 

 Usable capacity Provider-approved bookable slots after service duration, travel, geography and exclusions. One provider hour is not simultaneously available to multiple home visits or video calls. 

 Request acceptance Accepted valid requests / valid requests with an elapsed response window. Pending requests stay pending; duplicate/test requests excluded with reasons. 

 Fulfillment Completed episodes / confirmed episodes whose service and agreed reconciliation window have ended. Confirmed cancellations and no-shows stay in the denominator; open/future episodes are pending. Close after 48 hours unless a separately defined category window is agreed. 

 Provider cancellation Provider-caused cancellations / the same closed confirmed-episode cohort. Reason-code customer cancellation and suitability rejection separately; avoid moving inconvenient cases out of the cohort. 

 Arrival-window compliance Home visits starting within the displayed agreed arrival window / completed home visits with valid timestamps. Report delays, missing timestamps and sample size; no invented minute-perfect ETA. 

 Nonurgent support Eligible cases with first human response within 4 staffed hours / cases whose 4-staffed-hour deadline has elapsed. Customer and provider queues separate. After-hours time starts at the next published opening. A bot acknowledgement is not a human response. 

 Booking-critical acknowledgement Eligible current-service disruption cases acknowledged by a human within 15 staffed minutes / eligible cases with elapsed deadlines. Acknowledgement is not resolution; safety escalation starts immediately through the approved route. Do not advertise an unattended window. 

 Media-only first-completion CAC Customer media spend / attributed first-time customers with a completed eligible service in the same mature acquisition cohort. Use a disclosed 30-day acquisition window; show unattributed, pending and all-in cost separately. Do not divide by all appointments. 

 Provider acquisition cost Provider recruitment media / newly activated bookable providers attributed to that recruitment cohort. Track discussion→signed→activated lag; include field/production costs in a separate all-in view. 

 Household retention Households with another appropriate completed service within 90 days / first-completion households whose full 90 days have elapsed. No clinical justification inferred from repeat behavior. Do not count immature cohorts as failures. 

 Provider continuation Providers still verified, willing and offering usable capacity at day 90 / activated providers with an elapsed 90-day window. A dormant profile is not retained usable supply. 

 Commercial contribution Earned signed commission / agreed fees minus attributable processing, fulfillment subsidy, refunds borne, support and acquisition costs as defined. Calculate by category and mode; no blanket take-rate or annual forecast. 

 Settlement punctuality Eligible undisputed sums issued by the contractual due date / sums due. Issuance differs from clearance. Future-due collections are not overdue; explain permitted holds. 

 Every rate is shown with numerator, denominator, period and missing/pending counts. ## 12 How the service journeys should work

Seven proposed journeys, each with the booker, recipient, address and accountable professional or seller made explicit.

Design requirement, not a completed feature list The interactive journey selector shows the intended experience and the checks needed before release. No live booking, payment or clinical consultation is performed here.

 A customer must be able to tell the difference between a request, acceptance, payment and confirmation. I recommend reserving capacity, authorizing payment and capturing after provider acceptance where the payment partner supports it. If the existing system instead collects before acceptance, it must explicitly show pending status and provide tested rejection/refund handling. Finance and technical owners choose one supported flow before pilot.

 A provider who rejects or does not respond within the agreed request window does not create a confirmed appointment. Release the slot hold and recover payment under the approved policy. Offer alternatives with customer agreement. Do not silently substitute the professional, service mode, pet/human service or service address.

 On completion, the professional records the service, finance reconciles the eligible transaction and the authorized customer receives a receipt and next-step information. Clinical records and diagnostic results go through the approved private route, with permissions independent of payment.

### Book for someone else

Booker / payer · son in the US · Recipient · mother in Egypt · Service address · her supported home address

1. Choose the recipient: Select “someone else”, identify the recipient and obtain the required agreement or representative authority. Do not assume a family relationship grants access.

2. Set the service address: Enter the mother’s address, access details and local contact. Coverage is calculated from this address, not the son’s GPS or card country.

3. Choose home or suitable video: Show eligible services and actual provider availability. A professional reviews suitability; video is offered only within its approved scope.

4. Review the full price: Display service, visit/travel charges, currency and cancellation rules. Show Egypt service time and the booker’s local time. International card and phone behavior need testing.

5. Request, accept, pay and confirm: Use one verified payment state machine. Preferred design is authorization then capture on acceptance where supported; otherwise disclose pay-pending-acceptance and automatic rejection/refund handling.

6. Prepare and deliver: Recipient receives permitted instructions and provider identity. Share booking updates with the booker only within agreed permissions; clinical information stays with authorized people.

7. Complete and follow through: Record completion, receipt, support access and any refund. Invite feedback from the appropriate person; no public health details.
